Operational Workflow

How Advance Billing Works

A frictionless, five-step billing journey from initial distributor onboarding to exporting verified, scannable A4 PDF invoices.

01
ONBOARDING

Create Your Distributor Profile

Register in less than two minutes with your username, email, phone number, and business details. After setting up your secure password, your dedicated distributor workspace is ready instantly without complex approval wait times.

Instant Activation
Direct access to all billing & customer modules
02
DATA SETUP

Add Customers & Product Catalogs

Seed your master directory with client contacts, addresses, and GSTIN numbers. Add inventory items with custom SKUs, tax categories, and default unit prices. Once saved, these details autofill your invoices with a single keystroke.

Centralized Catalog
Prevents duplicate entries and ensures consistent rates
03
CALCULATION

Assemble & Preview Dynamic Invoices

Select your customer and append line items with their quantities. The invoice engine calculates individual totals, sums subtotals, factors in GST rates, and applies discounts in real-time. Inspect the formatted draft before finalizing.

Zero Manual Errors
Automated GST and subtotal arithmetic engine
04
SECURITY

Dynamic QR Code Generation

Upon invoice creation, our backend compiles a cryptographic payload combining the invoice number, distributor id, client identity, gross amount, and issue date into an offline-verifiable matrix QR code, embedded cleanly on every document.

Scannable Verification
Compatible with mobile cameras and web decoder
05
EXPORT

Download & Print A4 PDF Invoices

Download standardized A4 PDF invoices anytime with one click. Invoices are archived permanently in your history, allowing you to re-download, preview QR codes, review status indicators, or print client copies at your convenience.

Print & Share Ready
Standardized A4 portrait formatting
